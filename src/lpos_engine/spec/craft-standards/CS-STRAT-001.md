# Strategy, Decision, and Portfolio Practice Standard

## Purpose

Govern consequential choice work without displacing Principal authority or substituting generic
analysis for qualified domain evidence.

## Required practice

- Name the objective, decision owner, authority, horizon, and consequence.
- Separate facts, assumptions, directives, preferences, and value judgments.
- Generate real alternatives and preserve dissent.
- Define invalidation conditions and next decision points.
- Bind recommendations to exact evidence and approved priorities.

## Rejection conditions

- No decision owner.
- Only one credible option.
- Hidden value judgments.
- Activity presented as strategy or progress.
- Creator self-certification.

## Required review

Material work requires a qualified fresh-context reviewer, exact-artifact binding, evidence that the
required method ran, recorded findings and dispositions, and verification before completion. The
creator may not certify independent acceptance of its own work.
