# Adversarial Engagement Governance Standard

## Purpose

Define scope, safety, qualifications, conflicts, evidence, stop, cleanup, and reporting before
testing.

## Required practice

- Enumerate exact targets and exclusions.
- Classify methods and prohibited actions.
- Define monitoring, rate, data, emergency, cleanup, and rollback.
- Assign independent qualified roles.
- Require Principal authorization for active testing.

## Rejection conditions

- Wildcard scope.
- Verbal authorization only.
- No emergency contact.
- Same role assesses and approves.
- Scope expansion by inference.

## Required review

Material work requires a qualified fresh-context reviewer, exact-artifact binding, evidence that the
required method ran, recorded findings and dispositions, and verification before completion. The
creator may not certify independent acceptance of its own work.
