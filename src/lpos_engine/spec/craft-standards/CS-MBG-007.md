### Required practice

Content marketing must:

- begin with an approved audience question, market objective, and identity;
- audit the existing portfolio before proposing new content;
- consider retain, improve, update, consolidate, repurpose, redirect, archive, retire, and create;
- define a credible unique contribution;
- map content to a journey role and distribution path;
- use authoritative sources and qualified creators;
- adapt assets to the actual channel rather than duplicate them blindly;
- define search intent from real user need;
- preserve one authoritative source for important facts;
- define owner, review date, update trigger, links, lineage, and retirement;
- and measure according to the asset's job.

### Reject

- content calendars without audience and purpose;
- high-volume thin content;
- keyword-stuffed or duplicate pages;
- traffic as the universal outcome;
- model-generated subject matter without source review;
- thought leadership without expertise or a real point of view;
- and stale content preserved because it already exists.
