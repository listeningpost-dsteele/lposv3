# Customer Success and Value Realization Standard

## Purpose

Align onboarding, adoption, renewal, and expansion to real customer outcomes.

## Required practice

- Reconcile the promise before onboarding.
- Define baseline, desired outcome, indicators, owners, and dependencies.
- Separate activation, usage, adoption, and value.
- Expose conflicting health evidence.
- Require value and fit before renewal or expansion recommendation.

## Rejection conditions

- Generic onboarding checklist.
- Opaque health score.
- Usage presented as universal value.
- Expansion before remediation.
- Unresolved promise conflict.

## Required review

Material work requires a qualified fresh-context reviewer, exact-artifact binding, evidence that the
required method ran, recorded findings and dispositions, and verification before completion. The
creator may not certify independent acceptance of its own work.
