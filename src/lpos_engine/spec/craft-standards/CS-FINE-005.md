# Pricing and Packaging Economics Standard

Pricing begins with verified product truth, buyer and payer context, value mechanism, unit economics, customer evidence, market context, operational feasibility, and decision authority.

Competitor prices are context, not proof. A value metric must align with customer value, be understandable, measurable, auditable, difficult to game, operationally feasible, and compatible with cost and capacity.

Packages use meaningful fences. Do not withhold basic security, privacy, accessibility, reliability, or recovery to manufacture a premium tier.

Define price, billing period, minimums, commitments, usage, overage, trials, credits, discounts, exceptions, renewal, migration, downgrade, cancellation, and customer-protection behavior.

Use controlled testing and guardrails when evidence is uncertain. Short-term conversion alone does not validate a pricing decision.
