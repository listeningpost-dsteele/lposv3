# Production Artifact and Release Verification Standard

## Purpose

Independently review exact rendered artifacts and audit exact release evidence.

## Required practice

- Bind baseline, artifact, viewport or medium, environment, and release identity.
- Execute real journeys, controls, links, commands, and delivery.
- Check accessibility, truth, content, visual, and interaction blockers.
- Audit raw gates and source-to-release identity.
- Verify rollback and authority.

## Rejection conditions

- Source-only artifact review.
- Fake proof.
- Automated scan as sole accessibility evidence.
- Self-attested gate.
- Auditor created candidate.

## Required review

Material work requires a qualified fresh-context reviewer, exact-artifact binding, evidence that the
required method ran, recorded findings and dispositions, and verification before completion. The
creator may not certify independent acceptance of its own work.
