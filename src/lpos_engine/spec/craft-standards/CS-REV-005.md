# Customer Support Operations and Case Integrity Standard

## Purpose

Ensure support cases are correctly identified, routed, evidenced, communicated, resolved, and
converted into systemic learning.

## Required practice

- Verify customer, entitlement, impact, and evidence.
- Use impact-based severity and one accountable owner.
- Preserve communication and state history.
- Verify resolution or accepted disposition.
- Feed recurring failure demand to owning guilds.

## Rejection conditions

- Closure for metrics.
- Transfer without ownership.
- Workaround called fix.
- Security or privacy issue left in general queue.
- Unauthorized customer communication or concession.

## Required review

Material work requires a qualified fresh-context reviewer, exact-artifact binding, evidence that the
required method ran, recorded findings and dispositions, and verification before completion. The
creator may not certify independent acceptance of its own work.
