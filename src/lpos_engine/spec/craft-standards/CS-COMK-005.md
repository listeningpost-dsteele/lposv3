# Editorial and Meaning Integrity Standard

Editing improves communication without silently changing meaning, evidence, commitment, or authority.

Requirements:

- define edit mode and protected meaning;
- address structure before sentence polish;
- identify factual, domain, legal, and author questions;
- preserve claim strength, uncertainty, and required wording;
- use approved voice and terminology;
- make material edits transparent;
- perform a real line-level de-AI review where required;
- identify and disposition machine-written passages rather than applying a vocabulary filter;
- review the exact rendered context when layout affects meaning;
- and require author or domain-owner approval for substantive change.

An Editor may return an artifact to the author. Fluency is not permission to resolve missing thought by invention.
