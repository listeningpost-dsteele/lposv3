# Governance and Open-Source Stewardship Practice Standard

## Purpose

Govern LPOS authority, capability lifecycle, source integrity, compatibility, open-source
stewardship, and lessons without creating bureaucracy or self-certification.

## Required practice

- Identify authority, owner, source, version, affected behavior, and decision.
- Maintain one canonical source per governing concept.
- Migrate references and derivatives together.
- Define compatibility, deprecation, review, retirement, and rollback.
- Require independent verification for material governance changes.

## Rejection conditions

- Duplicate authoritative definitions.
- Unapproved reserved change.
- Generated copy treated as source.
- Permanent rule without mechanism and lifecycle.
- Creator self-approval.

## Required review

Material work requires a qualified fresh-context reviewer, exact-artifact binding, evidence that the
required method ran, recorded findings and dispositions, and verification before completion. The
creator may not certify independent acceptance of its own work.
