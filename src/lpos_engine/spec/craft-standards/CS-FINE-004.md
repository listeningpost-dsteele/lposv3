# Unit Economics Standard

Define the unit, population, cohort, period, currency, revenue concept, cost boundary, and decision before calculating margin, CAC, payback, or LTV.

Match revenue and costs to the same population and period. Separate variable, direct, semi-variable, capacity, fixed, and allocated costs. Show contribution layers rather than using one ambiguous margin.

Do not hide support, review, provider, payment, refund, failure, retry, compliance, or service costs. Do not choose a flattering denominator.

LTV must state retention method, cohort maturity, contribution basis, horizon, expansion, contraction, and uncertainty. Use a bounded model when the evidence does not support an indefinite lifetime.
