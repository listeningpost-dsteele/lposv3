# Nonfunctional Assurance Standard

## Purpose

Verify performance, capacity, resilience, recovery, compatibility, and resource behavior under
representative conditions.

## Required practice

- Define outcome and threshold.
- Use representative workload and production-like environment.
- Measure distributions, saturation, errors, resources, and cost.
- Exercise stress, endurance, failure, and recovery as required.
- Compare baseline and prove restoration.

## Rejection conditions

- Average-only latency.
- Empty-data load test.
- Unmatched environment generalized to production.
- Failover untested.
- Destructive live test without authority.

## Required review

Material work requires a qualified fresh-context reviewer, exact-artifact binding, evidence that the
required method ran, recorded findings and dispositions, and verification before completion. The
creator may not certify independent acceptance of its own work.
