# Quality and Release Assurance Practice Standard

## Purpose

Govern independent, exact-candidate, criticality-weighted assurance across software and nonsoftware
artifacts.

## Required practice

- Bind assurance to approved intent and exact candidate.
- Select gates by consequence and change surface.
- Exercise real outcomes, states, failures, recovery, and delivery.
- Preserve creator-reviewer independence.
- Use reproducible raw evidence and stable findings.

## Rejection conditions

- Creator self-certification.
- Green unit suite as complete proof.
- Mock replaces real boundary.
- Evidence from different candidate.
- Absence of findings claimed as correctness.

## Required review

Material work requires a qualified fresh-context reviewer, exact-artifact binding, evidence that the
required method ran, recorded findings and dispositions, and verification before completion. The
creator may not certify independent acceptance of its own work.
