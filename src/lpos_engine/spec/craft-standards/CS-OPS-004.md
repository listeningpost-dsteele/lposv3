# Service Operations and Queue Management Standard

Define the service outcome, unit of work, demand sources, intake, routing, queues, priorities, WIP limits, aging, capacity, service levels, quality controls, escalation, and operating review.

Service levels must define clock start and stop, population, exclusions, calendar, target, owner, and action.

Use distributions and ranges where average performance hides material tail behavior.

Do not solve invalid demand, rework, or poor flow with headcount or automation before identifying the mechanism.
