### Required practice

A visual identity must:

- link to approved brand strategy;
- preserve approved equity;
- translate strategy into visual principles;
- compare genuinely distinct concept territories;
- test representative applications;
- define marks, typography, color, imagery, graphic language, motion, and identity architecture as applicable;
- include accessibility and production requirements;
- define asset governance and migration;
- inspect exact exports and applications;
- and receive qualified brand-design review.

### Reject

- logo-only systems;
- technology trend imitation;
- inaccessible palettes;
- decorative complexity without brand logic;
- identities that work only in a presentation;
- and asset systems another designer cannot reproduce.
