# Adversarial Finding Trust and Closure Standard

## Purpose

Require fresh-context reproduction, evidence-based severity, immutable trust state, and
mechanism-level remediation verification.

## Required practice

- Validate raw and target hashes.
- Reconstruct asset, actor, path, control failure, and impact.
- Reproduce safely.
- Review severity in context.
- Retest original path and variants against corrected target.

## Rejection conditions

- Reviewer conflict.
- Severity from generic score alone.
- Closure from code diff.
- Stale target.
- Risk accepted by reviewer.

## Required review

Material work requires a qualified fresh-context reviewer, exact-artifact binding, evidence that the
required method ran, recorded findings and dispositions, and verification before completion. The
creator may not certify independent acceptance of its own work.
