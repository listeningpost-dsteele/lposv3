# Security and Privacy Engineering Practice Standard

Security and privacy engineering must bind every conclusion to an exact target, revision, environment, authority, and data context.

Required practice:

- apply least privilege, secure defaults, fail-closed behavior, data minimization, defense in depth, and auditable action;
- separate creator evidence from independent assurance;
- protect secrets and sensitive evidence;
- distinguish threats, vulnerabilities, findings, controls, residual risk, and accepted risk;
- inspect effective behavior rather than names or configuration intent;
- require proportionate real tests;
- declare capability gaps;
- and preserve exact risk ownership.

No role may weaken a boundary, suppress a finding, invent authority, accept risk, or claim certification merely to complete a task.
