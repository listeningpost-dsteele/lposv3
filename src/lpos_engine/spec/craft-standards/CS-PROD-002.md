# Platform Product Management Standard

Treat a platform as a product only when it has identifiable consumers, recurring jobs, a coherent capability boundary, and evidence that shared delivery creates material leverage.

Define:

- Consumers
- Consumer jobs
- Current workarounds
- Capability contract
- Permissions
- Limits
- Failure behavior
- Observability
- Service expectations
- Support
- Compatibility
- Versioning
- Migration
- Deprecation
- Adoption
- Cost and operational burden
- Outcome evidence

Engineering owns technical architecture. Reliability owns operational proof. Security owns trust judgment. Finance owns validated economics. Platform Product Management owns the consumer and product contract.

Reject platform theater, abstractions without consumers, theoretical reuse, hidden migration cost, undocumented interfaces, reliability promises without operational agreement, and launch without adoption.
