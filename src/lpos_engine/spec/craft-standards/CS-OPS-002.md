# Operational Process and Service Design Standard

A material process or service must define:

- recurring outcome;
- process and decision owners;
- actors and authority;
- intake and eligibility;
- records of truth;
- states and transitions;
- handoffs and queues;
- priorities and service levels;
- controls and approvals;
- exceptions and escalation;
- completion postconditions;
- and measurement.

Current-state evidence must distinguish documented, observed, inferred, and unknown behavior.

A target operating model must compare alternatives, including no change and a simpler manual process.

Do not add process to compensate for unresolved policy, poor product behavior, missing system state, or avoidable communication failure.
