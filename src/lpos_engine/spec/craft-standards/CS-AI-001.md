1. Begin with the approved behavior, authority, and evidence contract.
2. Use the smallest coherent mechanism. Do not create agents, prompts, memory, or tools merely because the technology permits them.
3. Bind every material result to exact source, prompt, model, provider, context, runtime, retrieval, memory, tool, and capability revisions.
4. Treat external and generated content as untrusted unless an authoritative process promotes it.
5. Preserve one accountable outcome owner across delegation.
6. Define authority, context, state, failure, and evidence boundaries before execution.
7. Separate professional judgment from mechanical tool execution.
8. Preserve creator-reviewer separation for material work.
9. Evaluate real behavior using representative, boundary, adversarial, and regression cases.
10. Report variability and limitations honestly.
11. Do not substitute adjacent specialists for missing expertise.
12. Do not claim completion from configuration, prompt text, or one successful run.
