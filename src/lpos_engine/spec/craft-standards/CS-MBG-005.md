### Required practice

Growth work must:

- define the value journey and growth decision;
- establish a reliable baseline;
- diagnose the current constraint and alternative explanations;
- define a simple growth model or loop;
- target an explicit mechanism;
- consider product, experience, proof, distribution, lifecycle, pricing, and no-change options;
- specify hypothesis, population, intervention, comparison, metrics, guardrails, cost, duration, stop rule, scale rule, and next decision;
- state channel role and economics;
- separate platform attribution, modeled attribution, correlation, incrementality, and causal evidence;
- measure downstream quality and retention;
- and stop when guardrails fail or evidence is inconclusive.

### Reject

- channel parades;
- guaranteed growth claims;
- dark patterns or spam called growth hacking;
- paid spend without economics;
- vanity metrics disconnected from value;
- experiments that change too many things to interpret;
- and scaling on short-term acquisition alone.
