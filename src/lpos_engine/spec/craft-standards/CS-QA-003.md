# Acceptance Contract and Traceability Standard

## Purpose

Protect approved behavior from implementation-driven weakening and make it executable.

## Required practice

- Use authoritative domain intent.
- Cover actors, states, permissions, failures, recovery, data, and cross-cutting effects.
- Define observable oracles.
- Trace criteria to sources and tests.
- Freeze and control changes.

## Rejection conditions

- Criteria mirror implementation.
- Happy path only.
- Unresolved product decision invented.
- Criterion removed after failure.
- No source traceability.

## Required review

Material work requires a qualified fresh-context reviewer, exact-artifact binding, evidence that the
required method ran, recorded findings and dispositions, and verification before completion. The
creator may not certify independent acceptance of its own work.
