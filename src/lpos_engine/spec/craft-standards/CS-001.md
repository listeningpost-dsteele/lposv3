### Doctrine

Write for the specific audience, from the approved identity, using only the product truth and proof available. Copy is one person telling another person something useful and persuasive. It is not a model describing a system's insides or performing the style of marketing.

### Required inputs

No material copy without:

- identity and versioned voice specification;
- audience;
- current belief or behavior;
- desired belief or action;
- approved problem and outcome;
- approved positioning;
- product or offer truth;
- mechanism;
- proof;
- objections;
- limitations;
- exact surface and channel;
- next action;
- and required reviews.

### Four mandatory tests

1. **Swap test:** if a competitor can paste the passage unchanged, rewrite it.
2. **Out-loud test:** if the approved identity would not plausibly say it to a person, rewrite it.
3. **Count test:** use a number only when it changes the audience's decision; otherwise explain its consequence or place inventory in factual material.
4. **So-what test:** every sentence answers a real audience question, supports credibility, or enables the next action.

### Banned or presumptively rejected patterns

- decorative number parades;
- decorative triads;
- antithesis reflexes such as “not just X, but Y”;
- mirrored sentence runs;
- rhetorical-question openers;
- “imagine a world” framing;
- generic transformation claims;
- title-case headline stacks;
- exclamation marks used to manufacture energy;
- uniform paragraph rhythm;
- unsupported superlatives;
- internal agent or workflow language without audience relevance;
- and generic words such as seamless, effortless, unlock, supercharge, elevate, empower, game-changing, revolutionize, delve, robust, cutting-edge, “in today's world,” and “look no further,” unless the approved identity and evidence support a rare deliberate use.

### Required process

1. Draft for substance against the brief and voice.
2. Trace every material claim.
3. Perform the creator's four-test and banned-pattern pass.
4. Inspect copy in the exact rendered artifact or sequence.
5. Send to a separate Editor for line-level de-AI and clarity review.
6. Send to a fresh-context commercial reviewer who quotes and adjudicates the three passages most likely to be generic, unsupported, or machine-written.
7. Obtain all triggered domain and policy review.

### Completion blocker

Copy is blocked when the voice, product truth, proof, claim authority, exact surface, or required review is missing.
