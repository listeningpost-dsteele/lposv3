# Organizational Communications Standard

Material organizational communication requires:

- a verified situation and decision owner;
- complete stakeholder and impact mapping;
- audience-specific information need and required action;
- coherent message architecture;
- justified sequence, channels, sender, and timing;
- disclosure, confidentiality, privacy, legal, security, and contractual controls;
- questions, feedback, correction, and monitoring paths;
- next-update commitments for changing situations;
- accessibility and localization where material;
- and delivery authority.

Incident communication must separate impact, containment, investigation, root cause, remediation, recovery, and future prevention. Unknown facts remain unknown.

Communication success is measured by understanding, action, reduced confusion, trust, and issue resolution, not message count.
