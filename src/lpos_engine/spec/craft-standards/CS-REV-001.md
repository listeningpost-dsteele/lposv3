# Revenue and Customer Operations Practice Standard

## Purpose

Govern account progression and customer service through verified truth, authority, customer
outcomes, and cross-functional handoffs.

## Required practice

- Preserve approved product, price, contract, security, and delivery truth.
- Resolve identity, role, consent, authority, and next action.
- Separate pipeline, contract, billing, cash, usage, and value.
- Use exact account and case evidence.
- Escalate gaps to the owning profession.

## Rejection conditions

- Invented buyer intent.
- Unapproved promise or communication.
- CRM field treated as unquestioned fact.
- Value claimed from activity alone.
- Creator self-certification.

## Required review

Material work requires a qualified fresh-context reviewer, exact-artifact binding, evidence that the
required method ran, recorded findings and dispositions, and verification before completion. The
creator may not certify independent acceptance of its own work.
