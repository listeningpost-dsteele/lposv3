# Constitutional, Policy, and Authority Integrity Standard

## Purpose

Preserve governing hierarchy, precedence, exceptions, approvals, and runtime inheritance.

## Required practice

- Classify the correct layer.
- Map precedence and conflicts.
- Require exact approval and effective date.
- Keep exceptions scoped and expiring.
- Verify exact compiled and runtime behavior.

## Rejection conditions

- Lower layer overrides higher authority.
- Silent conflict.
- Authority inferred from convenience.
- Policy text changed without behavior proof.
- Emergency exception becomes permanent.

## Required review

Material work requires a qualified fresh-context reviewer, exact-artifact binding, evidence that the
required method ran, recorded findings and dispositions, and verification before completion. The
creator may not certify independent acceptance of its own work.
