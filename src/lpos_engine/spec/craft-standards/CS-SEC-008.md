# Security Detection, Incident Response, and Forensics Readiness Standard

Detections begin with material threat scenarios and required response, not available telemetry. Every alert must have defined semantics, owner, severity, triage, false-positive conditions, containment options, and runbook.

Incident work must separate facts from hypotheses, preserve evidence, maintain a timeline, bind actions to authority, compare containment options, verify recovery, identify causal control failures, and assign corrective actions with closure tests.

Do not claim forensic sufficiency without the required qualification, collection integrity, and chain-of-custody controls.
