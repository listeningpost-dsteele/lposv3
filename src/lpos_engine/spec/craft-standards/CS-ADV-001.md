# Adversarial Security Assurance Practice Standard

## Purpose

Govern independent, exact-target, authorized, safe, untrusted-first adversarial work.

## Required practice

- Verify ownership, authority, target, mode, and scope.
- Use passive least-invasive methods first.
- Minimize and redact evidence.
- Separate assessor, remediator, reviewer, risk owner, and release authority.
- Report coverage, limitations, and residual uncertainty.

## Rejection conditions

- Unauthorized target.
- Raw finding presented as trusted.
- Sensitive evidence copied.
- Self-remediation and closure.
- No findings claimed as security.

## Required review

Material work requires a qualified fresh-context reviewer, exact-artifact binding, evidence that the
required method ran, recorded findings and dispositions, and verification before completion. The
creator may not certify independent acceptance of its own work.
