# Legal Research and Issue Analysis Standard

## Purpose

Produce proposition-level legal research and bounded analysis suitable for decision or counsel
review.

## Required practice

- Freeze facts and subissues.
- Apply authority hierarchy and effective dates.
- Analyze elements, definitions, thresholds, exceptions, and contrary authority.
- Bind propositions to sources.
- State missing facts and closure status.

## Rejection conditions

- Search snippets as law.
- Secondary source replacing controlling authority.
- Fact assumptions hidden.
- Adverse source omitted.
- Definitive conclusion beyond qualification.

## Required review

Material work requires a qualified fresh-context reviewer, exact-artifact binding, evidence that the
required method ran, recorded findings and dispositions, and verification before completion. The
creator may not certify independent acceptance of its own work.
