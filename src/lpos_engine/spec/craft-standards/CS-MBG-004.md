### Required practice

Product marketing must:

- link every claim to verified current product truth or an explicitly labeled approved future commitment;
- distinguish buyer, user, operator, influencer, approver, and blocker where relevant;
- use market evidence to select a specific priority audience;
- define the problem, alternative, frame, differentiated value, mechanism, proof, and tradeoff;
- build a message hierarchy rather than a feature list;
- define objections, limitations, and prohibited claims;
- maintain a claim and proof matrix;
- diagnose adoption barriers;
- assess launch readiness across product, proof, pricing, support, operations, policy, and measurement;
- create only enablement with a named user and purpose;
- and require market-facing outcome review.

### Reject

- generic SaaS positioning;
- roadmap claims presented as current;
- one message for every audience;
- launches driven only by dates;
- internal architecture presented as value without relevance;
- and messaging used to hide product defects.
