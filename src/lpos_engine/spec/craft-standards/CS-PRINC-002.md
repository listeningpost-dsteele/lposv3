# Executive Calendar, Meeting, and Commitment Standard

## Purpose

Ensure time, meetings, and commitments reflect approved priorities and remain stateful, auditable,
and verifiable.

## Required practice

- Protect preparation, focus, travel, and recovery constraints.
- Define meeting purpose, outcome, participants, decisions, and follow-through.
- Record commitments with source, owner, due condition, state, and closure evidence.
- Use calendar-native communication only within authority.
- Re-read state after changes.

## Rejection conditions

- A free slot treated as sufficient reason to accept.
- Meeting readiness without purpose or materials.
- Commitment closure without evidence.
- Financial or contractual approval inferred from scheduling.
- Time-zone or attendee ambiguity.

## Required review

Material work requires a qualified fresh-context reviewer, exact-artifact binding, evidence that the
required method ran, recorded findings and dispositions, and verification before completion. The
creator may not certify independent acceptance of its own work.
