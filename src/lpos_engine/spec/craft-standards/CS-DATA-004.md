# Decision Analysis and Reproducibility Standard

## Purpose

Answer bounded descriptive and diagnostic questions with reproducible evidence and disciplined
language.

## Required practice

- Predefine material analysis.
- Validate definitions and data.
- Inspect distributions, segments, missingness, and alternatives.
- Quantify uncertainty.
- Freeze inputs, code, environment, and outputs.

## Rejection conditions

- Dashboard screenshot as analysis.
- Average hides distribution.
- Post hoc segment presented as planned.
- Causal overclaim.
- Inconvenient outlier removed without rule.

## Required review

Material work requires a qualified fresh-context reviewer, exact-artifact binding, evidence that the
required method ran, recorded findings and dispositions, and verification before completion. The
creator may not certify independent acceptance of its own work.
