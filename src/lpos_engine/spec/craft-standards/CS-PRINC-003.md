# Relationship Intelligence and Stakeholder Context Standard

## Purpose

Provide verified relationship context without inventing motives, sentiment, authority, or
familiarity.

## Required practice

- Resolve identity before merging context.
- Preserve dated sources and direct statements.
- Label inference and unknowns.
- Respect privacy, consent, and channel expectations.
- Tie recommendations to an explicit relationship objective.

## Rejection conditions

- Relationship scoring without a validated purpose and method.
- Motive or sentiment presented as fact.
- Stale role treated as current.
- Private context disclosed beyond need-to-know.
- Account metrics used as personal relationship truth.

## Required review

Material work requires a qualified fresh-context reviewer, exact-artifact binding, evidence that the
required method ran, recorded findings and dispositions, and verification before completion. The
creator may not certify independent acceptance of its own work.
