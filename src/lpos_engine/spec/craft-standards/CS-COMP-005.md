# Compliance Program and External Assessment Standard

## Purpose

Run framework programs and coordinate external assessors without compromising independence or
overstating status.

## Required practice

- Define scope, criteria, report type, period, owners, and authority.
- Separate documented, implemented, evidenced, tested, effective, and externally assessed.
- Track assessor requests and findings exactly.
- Prepare management inputs for authorized human approval.
- Control report dates, limitations, and public claims.

## Rejection conditions

- Readiness called certification.
- Assessor finding edited.
- Management representation signed by model.
- Expired report claim.
- Scope exclusions hidden.

## Required review

Material work requires a qualified fresh-context reviewer, exact-artifact binding, evidence that the
required method ran, recorded findings and dispositions, and verification before completion. The
creator may not certify independent acceptance of its own work.
