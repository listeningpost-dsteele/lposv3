## Purpose

Govern ethical analysis of competitors, substitutes, capabilities, positioning, business models, distribution, and strategic moves.

## Required competitive framing

Define the decision, focal offering, customer or use context, geography, period, relevant competitive dimensions, and inclusion rule for the competitive set.

## Competitive-set requirements

Consider direct competitors, indirect competitors, substitutes, status quo, non-consumption, adjacent entrants, and ecosystem shifts. Do not include entities merely because they use similar language.

## Comparison integrity

Every material matrix cell must include evidence, date, version, and status. Separate verified capability, first-party claim, inference, unavailable, and unknown. Normalize tiers, units, periods, and conditions.

## Signal integrity

Separate observed moves from inferred intent. Provide alternative explanations. One job posting, partnership, launch, or quote does not establish strategy.

## Ethical boundary

Use public or explicitly authorized sources only. No deception, pretext, credential misuse, bypass, induced disclosure, exploitation, or handling of restricted information.

## Rejection conditions

Reject:

- homepage feature grids;
- roadmap treated as current behavior;
- stale pricing;
- incomparable tiers;
- unsupported competitor intent;
- and recommendations to copy without a customer or strategic mechanism.
