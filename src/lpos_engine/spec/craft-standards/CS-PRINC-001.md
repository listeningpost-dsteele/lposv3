# Principal Operations Practice Standard

## Purpose

Govern executive-office work that supports the Principal without displacing Chip or inventing
personal authority.

## Required practice

- Resolve authority and identity before acting.
- Use authoritative source state, not remembered summaries.
- Separate fact, stated preference, inference, recommendation, and Principal decision.
- Minimize personal context and preserve provenance.
- Verify the actual resulting state and delivery.

## Rejection conditions

- Unresolved authority.
- Invented preference or relationship claim.
- State change verified only by the creator’s statement.
- Sensitive context exposed beyond task need.
- Office work that substitutes for another profession.

## Required review

Material work requires a qualified fresh-context reviewer, exact-artifact binding, evidence that the
required method ran, recorded findings and dispositions, and verification before completion. The
creator may not certify independent acceptance of its own work.
