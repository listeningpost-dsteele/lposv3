# Commercial Contract Review and Obligation Standard

## Purpose

Make exact contract rights, duties, deviations, remedies, approvals, and lifecycle operational.

## Required practice

- Verify complete exact document set and order of precedence.
- Extract definitions, obligations, rights, conditions, remedies, and dates.
- Compare approved positions and route domain commitments.
- Preserve redline meaning.
- Track post-signature obligations and renewal.

## Rejection conditions

- Summary-only review.
- Linked terms ignored.
- Signature without unresolved deviations.
- Operational promise without owner validation.
- Auto-renewal or notice date omitted.

## Required review

Material work requires a qualified fresh-context reviewer, exact-artifact binding, evidence that the
required method ran, recorded findings and dispositions, and verification before completion. The
creator may not certify independent acceptance of its own work.
