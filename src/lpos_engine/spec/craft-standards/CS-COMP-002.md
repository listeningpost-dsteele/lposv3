# Control Framework and Design Assurance Standard

## Purpose

Create testable, owned, precise, reusable controls mapped to applicable obligations.

## Required practice

- Normalize obligations.
- Define objective, risk, actor, action, condition, frequency, population, evidence, reviewer, exception, and owner.
- Assess coverage, precision, bypass, and segregation.
- Reuse canonical controls across frameworks.
- Record gaps explicitly.

## Rejection conditions

- Framework text copied as control.
- Ownerless control.
- No population or evidence.
- Operator self-review without rationale.
- Gap silently omitted.

## Required review

Material work requires a qualified fresh-context reviewer, exact-artifact binding, evidence that the
required method ran, recorded findings and dispositions, and verification before completion. The
creator may not certify independent acceptance of its own work.
