# Legal and Regulatory Practice Standard

## Purpose

Govern legal preparation with jurisdiction, exact facts, authoritative sources, qualification,
uncertainty, counsel escalation, and action authority.

## Required practice

- State jurisdiction, parties, facts, issue, authority, and intended use.
- Separate law, contract, policy, guidance, risk, and preference.
- Use exact current authoritative sources.
- Preserve contrary authority and uncertainty.
- Escalate reserved or high-consequence matters to qualified counsel.

## Rejection conditions

- No jurisdiction.
- Outdated or nonauthoritative source.
- Binding action without authority.
- Model represented as counsel.
- Uncertainty edited away.

## Required review

Material work requires a qualified fresh-context reviewer, exact-artifact binding, evidence that the
required method ran, recorded findings and dispositions, and verification before completion. The
creator may not certify independent acceptance of its own work.
