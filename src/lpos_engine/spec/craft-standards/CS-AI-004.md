Define the capability before selecting candidates. Evaluate exact model, provider, API, and configuration revisions using representative role tasks.

Measure:

- professional artifact quality;
- instruction following;
- tool use;
- structured output;
- context handling;
- grounding and citation behavior;
- boundary and refusal behavior;
- reliability and variance;
- latency distributions;
- quotas and provider failure;
- cost under actual workload;
- privacy, residency, and data-use fit;
- fallback integrity;
- operational lifecycle.

Reject:

- provider marketing as evidence;
- popularity routing;
- one-run conclusions;
- incomparable configurations;
- hidden model revisions;
- benchmark leakage;
- fallback that violates policy or capability;
- universal-best claims.
