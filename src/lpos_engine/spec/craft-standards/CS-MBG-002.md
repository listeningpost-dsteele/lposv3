### Required practice

A brand strategy must:

- identify the exact identity and owner;
- define the decision and reason it is needed;
- inspect existing equity and current associations;
- use trustworthy audience and market evidence;
- define the relevant frame or category;
- make a meaningful positioning choice and tradeoff;
- articulate a credible durable promise;
- trace reasons to believe;
- define architecture among company, product, program, open-source, and Principal identities;
- translate personality into observable behavior;
- define identity-specific voice and narrative boundaries;
- consider no change and narrower alternatives;
- establish measurement and review;
- and require the proper authority for material change.

### Reject

- three-adjective brand strategies;
- trend-driven rebrands;
- unsupported transformation promises;
- copied competitor positions;
- visual design presented as strategy;
- company and Principal voice conflation;
- and identity proliferation without need.
