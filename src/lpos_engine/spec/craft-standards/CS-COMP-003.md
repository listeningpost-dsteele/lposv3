# Control Evidence and Audit Workpaper Standard

## Purpose

Require authentic, complete, reproducible evidence and defensible testing.

## Required practice

- Use native evidence with provenance.
- Establish complete population.
- Select reproducible sample or full population.
- Reperform control logic.
- Document procedure, evidence, exceptions, and conclusion.

## Rejection conditions

- Screenshot only.
- Owner-created pass spreadsheet.
- Sample excludes failures.
- Evidence generated after request.
- Auditor operates control.

## Required review

Material work requires a qualified fresh-context reviewer, exact-artifact binding, evidence that the
required method ran, recorded findings and dispositions, and verification before completion. The
creator may not certify independent acceptance of its own work.
