# Strategic Choice and Adaptive Planning Standard

## Purpose

Require strategy to contain diagnosis, focused choices, advantage or leverage, non-choices,
capability requirements, and adaptive review.

## Required practice

- Diagnose the causal challenge.
- State focus and non-choices.
- Explain why the approach can create value or advantage.
- Identify capabilities, dependencies, and constraints.
- Use milestones as evidence and define triggers.

## Rejection conditions

- Aspiration without choice.
- Roadmap without mechanism.
- Every opportunity included.
- Copied competitor strategy.
- No review or invalidation logic.

## Required review

Material work requires a qualified fresh-context reviewer, exact-artifact binding, evidence that the
required method ran, recorded findings and dispositions, and verification before completion. The
creator may not certify independent acceptance of its own work.
