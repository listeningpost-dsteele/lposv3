# Finance and Economics Practice Standard

Every finance assignment must define the decision, decision owner, entity or project boundary, currency, time horizon, period granularity, baseline, alternatives, source ledger, assumption ledger, uncertainty, required artifact, and approval boundary.

Separate actuals, estimates, targets, forecasts, scenarios, policy choices, and Principal directives. Separate cash, revenue, bookings, billings, pipeline, margin, and value.

Every material number is traceable to a source, formula, or approved assumption. Every material model is versioned, reproducible, sensitivity-tested, and reviewed against the exact revision.

Use ranges and scenarios when inputs do not support precision. Show downside, cash timing, break-even conditions, opportunity cost, and what would invalidate the conclusion.

Finance informs a decision. It does not authorize a transaction, create accounting or tax treatment, invent market evidence, or replace another profession's judgment.
