# Financial Planning and Forecasting Standard

Budgets and forecasts must reconcile to an explicit baseline, use decision-relevant drivers, identify commitments, distinguish targets from forecasts, and show cash timing where material.

A runway result must use a cash schedule when burn, collections, commitments, or one-time events are materially variable. A single recent burn rate is not a universal forecast.

Variance analysis identifies amount, timing, driver, controllability, permanence, future impact, and decision required. Lower current spend is not savings when the obligation is merely delayed.

Material forecasts include credible adverse and favorable cases, sensitivities, trigger points, and an exact review record.
