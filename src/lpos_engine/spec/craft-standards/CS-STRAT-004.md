# Scenario and Strategic Risk Standard

## Purpose

Stress-test choices against causal uncertainty and make adaptation operational.

## Required practice

- Choose material unresolved uncertainties.
- Build distinct causal scenarios.
- Map exposures, propagation, concentration, and second-order effects.
- Define observable indicators and owners.
- Tie triggers to preplanned decisions or contingencies.

## Rejection conditions

- Percentage-only best/base/worst.
- Generic risk list.
- False probabilities.
- No trigger owner.
- Contingency after irreversibility.

## Required review

Material work requires a qualified fresh-context reviewer, exact-artifact binding, evidence that the
required method ran, recorded findings and dispositions, and verification before completion. The
creator may not certify independent acceptance of its own work.
