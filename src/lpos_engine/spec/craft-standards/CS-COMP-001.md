# Compliance and Control Assurance Practice Standard

## Purpose

Govern framework-independent, independent control assurance and preserve the boundary with legal
interpretation, creator operation, risk acceptance, and external attestation.

## Required practice

- Map approved obligations to canonical controls.
- Separate design, implementation, evidence, operation, internal testing, and external opinion.
- Use native evidence, complete populations, and exact periods.
- Preserve exceptions and deficiencies.
- Require independent reviewers and accurate claim language.

## Rejection conditions

- Framework checklist as assurance.
- Policy as operation proof.
- Self-audit.
- Preset pass result.
- Internal readiness called external compliance.

## Required review

Material work requires a qualified fresh-context reviewer, exact-artifact binding, evidence that the
required method ran, recorded findings and dispositions, and verification before completion. The
creator may not certify independent acceptance of its own work.
