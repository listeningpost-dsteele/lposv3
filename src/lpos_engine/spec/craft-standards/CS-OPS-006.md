# Operational Excellence and Improvement Standard

Begin with a defined performance decision and valid baseline.

Inspect the real process and distinguish:

- demand from failure demand;
- wait from touch time;
- symptom from constraint;
- variation from error;
- local performance from end-to-end outcome;
- capacity released from cash saved;
- and correlation from causal effect.

Prefer removing, simplifying, or standardizing before adding tools, meetings, approvals, reports, or automation.

Every material improvement must define a falsifiable hypothesis, owner, population, intervention, guardrails, success criteria, stop criteria, duration, evidence, and reversal path.
