# Assurance Criticality and Release Evidence Standard

## Purpose

Require a complete gauntlet and evidence architecture appropriate to release risk.

## Required practice

- Classify criticality from consequence, novelty, data, authority, reversibility, and blast radius.
- Define required roles, skills, environments, and evidence.
- Track findings and exceptions.
- Bind packet to immutable identities.
- Audit all gates before release.

## Rejection conditions

- Criticality based only on diff size.
- Gate waived by creator.
- Summary without raw evidence.
- Unowned exception.
- Release recommendation before audit.

## Required review

Material work requires a qualified fresh-context reviewer, exact-artifact binding, evidence that the
required method ran, recorded findings and dispositions, and verification before completion. The
creator may not certify independent acceptance of its own work.
