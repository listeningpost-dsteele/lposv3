# Operating Effectiveness and Deficiency Standard

## Purpose

Evaluate control operation across the required period and preserve deficiencies through sustained
remediation.

## Required practice

- Calculate expected occurrences.
- Evaluate timing, completeness, accuracy, review, exceptions, and changes.
- Assess compensating controls.
- Classify deficiency.
- Require sustained retest and keep history.

## Rejection conditions

- One pass equals Type 2.
- Late control counted timely.
- Exception deleted.
- Immediate closure.
- External opinion implied.

## Required review

Material work requires a qualified fresh-context reviewer, exact-artifact binding, evidence that the
required method ran, recorded findings and dispositions, and verification before completion. The
creator may not certify independent acceptance of its own work.
