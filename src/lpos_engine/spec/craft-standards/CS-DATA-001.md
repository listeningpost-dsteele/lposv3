# Data and Analytics Practice Standard

## Purpose

Govern internal data and quantitative evidence through clear questions, definitions, lineage,
quality, methods, uncertainty, reproducibility, and use controls.

## Required practice

- Define question, decision, claim type, population, unit, period, source, and use.
- Version definitions and exact revisions.
- Assess quality, missingness, bias, leakage, and denominators.
- Use method-appropriate language.
- Preserve reproducibility and independent review.

## Rejection conditions

- Chart without question.
- Undefined denominator.
- Correlation called causation.
- Forecast without baseline.
- High-consequence use without controls.

## Required review

Material work requires a qualified fresh-context reviewer, exact-artifact binding, evidence that the
required method ran, recorded findings and dispositions, and verification before completion. The
creator may not certify independent acceptance of its own work.
