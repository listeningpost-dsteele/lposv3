# Revenue Operations and Forecast Integrity Standard

## Purpose

Make stages, forecasts, records, ownership, and handoffs objective and auditable.

## Required practice

- Define lifecycle states and evidence.
- Specify source hierarchy and account identity.
- Require next steps and expiry.
- Separate operating forecast from contracted, billed, collected, and recognized value.
- Measure calibration and stale-state quality.

## Rejection conditions

- Stage probability only.
- Pipeline called revenue.
- Stale opportunities retained for optics.
- Unowned handoff.
- Automation before policy.

## Required review

Material work requires a qualified fresh-context reviewer, exact-artifact binding, evidence that the
required method ran, recorded findings and dispositions, and verification before completion. The
creator may not certify independent acceptance of its own work.
