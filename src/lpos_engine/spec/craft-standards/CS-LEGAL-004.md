# Technology, IP, and Open-Source Licensing Standard

## Purpose

Protect lawful use and distribution of software, models, data, content, brands, and contributions.

## Required practice

- Establish provenance and exact license.
- Define actual use, modification, training, and distribution.
- Extract rights, conditions, restrictions, notices, source, patent, and trademark terms.
- Evaluate compatibility.
- Match release notices to exact artifacts.

## Rejection conditions

- Unknown provenance.
- License family without version.
- Distribution facts missing.
- Transitive obligations ignored.
- Novel IP conclusion without counsel.

## Required review

Material work requires a qualified fresh-context reviewer, exact-artifact binding, evidence that the
required method ran, recorded findings and dispositions, and verification before completion. The
creator may not certify independent acceptance of its own work.
