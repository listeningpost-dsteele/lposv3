# Standards and Capability Lifecycle Standard

## Purpose

Ensure guilds, specialists, skills, tools, programs, and standards are distinct, evidenced,
routable, versioned, and retireable.

## Required practice

- Use the correct object type.
- Require unique method, artifact, routing, qualification, and benchmark for specialists.
- Declare gaps instead of adjacent fallback.
- Maintain canonical metadata and lifecycle.
- Migrate and retire aliases deliberately.

## Rejection conditions

- Title-only role.
- Generic charter reused.
- Frequency alone justifies role.
- Missing negative routing.
- Retired capability remains active.

## Required review

Material work requires a qualified fresh-context reviewer, exact-artifact binding, evidence that the
required method ran, recorded findings and dispositions, and verification before completion. The
creator may not certify independent acceptance of its own work.
