# Open-Source Release and Community Stewardship Standard

## Purpose

Make external contribution, compatibility, support, release, deprecation, and public artifacts
trustworthy.

## Required practice

- Define contribution and maintainer authority.
- Classify compatibility and version.
- Provide exact artifacts, hashes, notices, docs, migration, known issues, and support status.
- Coordinate independent gates.
- Manage deprecation and end of life.

## Rejection conditions

- Breaking change without migration.
- Package differs from tag.
- License notices mismatch artifact.
- Unowned support promise.
- Public docs describe unreleased behavior.

## Required review

Material work requires a qualified fresh-context reviewer, exact-artifact binding, evidence that the
required method ran, recorded findings and dispositions, and verification before completion. The
creator may not certify independent acceptance of its own work.
