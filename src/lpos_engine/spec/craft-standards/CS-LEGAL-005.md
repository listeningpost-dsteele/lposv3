# Privacy and Product Regulatory Analysis Standard

## Purpose

Translate qualified privacy and product law into observable requirements without conflating legal
interpretation, engineering, and assurance.

## Required practice

- Map product, actors, data, behavior, and jurisdictions.
- Analyze definitions, thresholds, roles, exemptions, and territorial scope.
- Extract obligations, prohibitions, notices, choices, rights, records, and effective dates.
- Create product requirements and counsel flags.
- Keep compliance claims within evidence.

## Rejection conditions

- Policy text used as compliance proof.
- One jurisdiction generalized globally.
- Technical control treated as legal opinion.
- Consent assumed without purpose analysis.
- Regulator action without authority.

## Required review

Material work requires a qualified fresh-context reviewer, exact-artifact binding, evidence that the
required method ran, recorded findings and dispositions, and verification before completion. The
creator may not certify independent acceptance of its own work.
