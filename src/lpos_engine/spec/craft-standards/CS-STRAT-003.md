# Decision Analysis and Decision Record Standard

## Purpose

Produce fair, explicit, traceable decisions under uncertainty.

## Required practice

- Freeze decision, owner, scope, and deadline.
- Compare feasible options at the same level.
- Separate must-haves, preferences, and Principal values.
- Use ranges, sensitivity, reversibility, and information value.
- Record choice, rationale, dissent, assumptions, and next review.

## Rejection conditions

- False binary.
- Arbitrary scoring weights.
- Predetermined conclusion.
- Sunk-cost reasoning.
- Record rewritten after outcome.

## Required review

Material work requires a qualified fresh-context reviewer, exact-artifact binding, evidence that the
required method ran, recorded findings and dispositions, and verification before completion. The
creator may not certify independent acceptance of its own work.
