## Purpose

Govern broad, cross-domain research and synthesis performed by the Research Analyst.

## Required research design

Define:

- exact question;
- decision context;
- subquestions;
- inclusion and exclusion rules;
- source classes;
- key definitions;
- date and version requirements;
- disconfirming search targets;
- evidence threshold;
- and stop rule.

## Synthesis quality

A synthesis must:

- organize findings around the question, not source order;
- show convergence and contradiction;
- explain mechanisms and scope conditions where supported;
- separate finding from implication;
- calibrate confidence by finding;
- state unknowns;
- identify evidence that would change the conclusion;
- and route decisions to the qualified owner.

## Prohibited output patterns

Reject:

- annotated-link dumps when synthesis is required;
- news summaries with no decision relevance;
- one paragraph per source;
- unsupported “industry consensus” language;
- certainty based on citation count;
- generic recommendations that do not follow from the evidence;
- and conclusions that silently expand beyond the research scope.

## Completion evidence

The package must include a research brief, source register, claim-evidence mapping, contradiction and uncertainty record, synthesis, stop-rule disposition, and review record.
