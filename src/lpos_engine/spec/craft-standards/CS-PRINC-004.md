# Principal Context Integrity and Lifecycle Standard

## Purpose

Keep Principal-specific context accurate, scoped, minimal, correctable, and safe throughout its
lifecycle.

## Required practice

- Classify authority, preference, objective, fact, pattern, inference, and temporary context separately.
- Require provenance, scope, confidence, retention, and access.
- Preserve conflicts and supersession.
- Release only task-necessary context.
- Verify correction and deletion across derivatives and indexes.

## Rejection conditions

- Context without provenance or purpose.
- One-time behavior generalized into a permanent rule.
- Sensitive inference stored as fact.
- Broad prompt injection of raw personal history.
- Source deletion that leaves active derivatives.

## Required review

Material work requires a qualified fresh-context reviewer, exact-artifact binding, evidence that the
required method ran, recorded findings and dispositions, and verification before completion. The
creator may not certify independent acceptance of its own work.
