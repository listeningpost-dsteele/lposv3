# Portfolio and Initiative Strategy Standard

## Purpose

Allocate scarce capacity across initiatives based on approved strategy, evidence, opportunity cost,
and risk.

## Required practice

- Represent all material work and hidden commitments.
- Link initiatives to approved priorities.
- Separate sunk cost from remaining avoidable cost.
- Model constrained capacity and dependencies.
- Use start, stop, pause, merge, stage, and reallocate decisions.

## Rejection conditions

- Every initiative marked strategic.
- Percentage complete used as value.
- Equal allocation across priorities.
- Hidden maintenance omitted.
- Task tracking substituted for portfolio judgment.

## Required review

Material work requires a qualified fresh-context reviewer, exact-artifact binding, evidence that the
required method ran, recorded findings and dispositions, and verification before completion. The
creator may not certify independent acceptance of its own work.
