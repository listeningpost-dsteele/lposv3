# Sales Qualification and Deal Integrity Standard

## Purpose

Pursue only evidence-supported, suitable opportunities and preserve every commercial commitment.

## Required practice

- Verify stakeholder roles and decision process.
- Define problem, outcome, urgency, fit, gaps, and alternatives.
- Create mutual actions.
- Require specialist validation for claims.
- Keep disqualification and no-sale legitimate.

## Rejection conditions

- Enthusiasm treated as commitment.
- Roadmap promise.
- Fake scarcity or proof.
- Discount without authority and rationale.
- Risks removed from handoff.

## Required review

Material work requires a qualified fresh-context reviewer, exact-artifact binding, evidence that the
required method ran, recorded findings and dispositions, and verification before completion. The
creator may not certify independent acceptance of its own work.
