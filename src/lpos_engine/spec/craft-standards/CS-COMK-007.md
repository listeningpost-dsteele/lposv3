# Information and Presentation Design Standard

Information design must help a defined audience understand, decide, remember, or act without distorting the underlying content.

Requirements:

- define audience, delivery context, desired understanding, and next action;
- choose the simplest appropriate artifact;
- establish a narrative spine before layout;
- give every slide or page one primary job;
- preserve source, scale, denominator, uncertainty, and causal limits;
- distinguish observed, modeled, proposed, and illustrative content;
- apply approved brand systems without sacrificing comprehension;
- design density, pacing, notes, and interaction for the real delivery mode;
- meet accessibility requirements;
- and render and inspect the exact output.

A deck that is a document divided into rectangles fails even when it is visually polished.
