# Experimentation and Causal Inference Standard

## Purpose

Estimate intervention effects through defensible identification, precommitment, implementation
integrity, and uncertainty.

## Required practice

- Define estimand and decision.
- Prefer randomization when ethical and feasible.
- Precommit outcomes, power, duration, stopping, and analysis.
- Test assignment, exposure, interference, and contamination.
- Report effect, uncertainty, heterogeneity, harms, and assumptions.

## Rejection conditions

- Before-after called causal.
- Outcome changed after results.
- Peeking without correction.
- Underpowered null called no effect.
- Harm ignored for power.

## Required review

Material work requires a qualified fresh-context reviewer, exact-artifact binding, evidence that the
required method ran, recorded findings and dispositions, and verification before completion. The
creator may not certify independent acceptance of its own work.
