# 4.7.0: guild and specialist expert capability system

LPOS 4.7.0 replaces generic specialist instructions with a complete expert capability system.

## What changed

- Twenty guild charters define shared professional doctrine, scope, authority, handoffs, review, and completion conditions.
- One hundred three specialist charters define role-specific methods, required inputs, artifacts, boundaries, failure patterns, and escalation.
- One hundred twenty-two craft standards provide reusable professional practice requirements.
- The active capability registry routes all 103 specialists with their guild and craft-standard bindings.
- A source-bound behavioral admission corpus adds one frozen suite for every specialist, with blinded candidate and generic-baseline support.

## Verification

- The deterministic admission harness, schemas, tamper checks, and release-integrity gates are included in the immutable distribution.
- A one-pass GLM guild sample compared one specialist from every guild against a capable generic baseline. Specialists won 16 of 20 blinded comparisons, with one tie.
- Four generated positive cases incorrectly required completed artifacts despite withholding the inputs that the charters require. Those failures remain recorded as admission-oracle defects rather than being hidden or used to weaken specialist evidence discipline.

## Preserved controls

- Product Studio remains excluded and deferred.
- Existing Principal authority, independent review, evidence, security, record-only external actions, and rollback controls remain in force.
